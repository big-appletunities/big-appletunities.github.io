About Us

Made by students, for students.

We started Big Appletunities because we wanted a simpler way to find and keep track of opportunities for high school students in NYC and around the world. We built the finder to be a simple, readable, and searchable database that can grow with our community's needs. Have an inquiry or suggestion? Reach out!

Email: bigappletunities@gmail.com Instagram: @bigappletunities Tiktok: @bigappletunities

Community

A tight-knit community of high school students, college students, and educators who share opportunities and feedback. Always open for expansion, just reach out!

Readable by design

Categories, timelines, and audience labels are structured so you can scan quickly and decide faster.

Expandable dataset

The finder is designed around CSV data, which keeps updates simple as the list grows.
