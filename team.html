Our team

Big Appletunities is made up of a group of passionate high school students from around the world. We are all dedicated to our mission of finding meaningful opportunities for students and making them accessible to everyone. Our team is made up of a director, editors, researchers, and promoters who work together to curate and share appletunities with our audience. We are committed to creating a supportive and inclusive community where students can discover and pursue their passions.
